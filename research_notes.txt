robotai - Dual-hand robotic project - Research Notes
======================================================

GOAL
----
Build a BOM (Bill of Materials) for two 3D-printed robotic hands (3D printing
is free/already covered, NOT part of this budget), each driven by its own
ESP32 dev board. Total budget for everything else: under $500 CAD.
User explicitly wants "a good hand" - not the cheapest/flimsiest option.
Final deliverable: an Excel BOM (Item, Product Name, Link, Unit Price CAD,
Quantity, Line Total, Notes) with a core total (must stay under $500 CAD) and
a clearly SEPARATE optional section for fingertip pressure/touch sensors
(FSRs) with its own subtotal.

DESIGN ASSUMPTIONS
-------------------
- 2 hands, standard InMoov-style tendon-driven design
- 1 ESP32 dev board PER hand (2 total, not shared)
- 5 servo motors per hand (10 total) - one per finger, tendon + spring return
- Servos need their OWN 5-6V power supply, NOT powered off the ESP32 5V pin
  (5 servos under load draw real current)
- Tendons: thin braided line (fishing line), one shared spool likely covers
  both hands
- Springs: small extension/return springs, enough for 10 fingers total
- Optional: FSR fingertip pressure sensors, e.g. 2 units (one per hand,
  proof-of-concept), kept as a separate optional line item/subtotal

KNOWN BLOCKER
-------------
Amazon.ca blocks plain WebFetch/automated page fetches (returns HTTP 503).
Real product data can only be pulled by rendering the page in an actual
browser (e.g. browser automation tools), not a simple fetch. DigiKey.ca
fetches were noted as working reliably in an earlier attempt, so it's a good
alternative/fallback source for the electronic components (servo driver
board, buck converter, connectors) if Amazon browsing is inconvenient.

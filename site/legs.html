The legs

The InMoov gets legs in three stages. It learns to walk in simulation first, a small real walker proves the code at week 10, and full-size legs go under the InMoov in v2. The legs shown are that v2 concept, based on the open Berkeley Humanoid Lite design scaled to InMoov size. Point at a part to light it up.

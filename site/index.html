A life-size humanoid that runs your home.

Our own 3D-printed humanoid design, with five-finger hands, that cooks, tidies and fetches, and one day walks the dog. It rolls on a wheeled base while its walking legs are trained in simulation. It learns chores from demonstrations, and a fast multimodal model on Cerebras does the thinking. Built by students in Toronto.

The arm

Each arm is life-size, copied from the InMoov design: a printed shell, strong self-locking motors at the shoulder and elbow, five finger servos hidden in the forearm, and tendons that pull the fingers. Point at a part to light it up.

    How the hand fits together

    The right forearm, wrist and hand we're printing first. Press Take apart to pull every piece away from the others, then put it back together. Hover a piece to see what it is. The left hand is a mirror copy.

    Loading the hand…
    • Forearm 11 printed pieces. The 5 finger servos sit on the servo bed inside it and pull the tendons.
    • Wrist 5 pieces: a geared ring turned by one more servo.
    • Hand 11 pieces: palm halves, back covers and 5 fingers, each finger hinged on M3 bolts.
    • Tendons 200 lb braided line, two per finger (one closes it, one opens it), running through PTFE tube.
